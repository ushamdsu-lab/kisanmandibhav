User Data Deletion Instructions

Google Play Store Data Safety Compliance | Last updated: September 20, 2026
Your Data Privacy: Kisan Mandi Bhav & Weather is designed with a strict privacy-first architecture. We do not require account registration or collect passwords, phone numbers, or bank account credentials.

1. How Data is Stored in Our App

The app allows you to record entries in the Farm Khata (Income/Expenses) and Dairy Tracker. All of this information is saved strictly on your local device storage (SQLite/SharedPreferences). It is never sent, synced, or backed up to external servers owned by us.

2. Immediate Self-Service Data Deletion (Step-by-Step)

Because your data is stored locally on your own device, you can completely and permanently delete all your data instantly without needing to contact us:

  1. Open your Android phone's Settings app.
  2. Tap on Apps (or Apps & Notifications > See all apps).
  3. Scroll and select Kisan Mandi Bhav.
  4. Tap on Storage & Cache.
  5. Tap Clear Storage (or Clear Data) and confirm.

Result: All your local Farm Khata records, preferences, cached mandi prices, and temporary files will be permanently wiped from your phone immediately.

3. Resetting Advertising ID

To delete or reset the advertising identifier used by Google AdMob on your device:

  1. Go to Android Settings > Google > Ads.
  2. Tap Delete advertising ID or Reset advertising ID.
