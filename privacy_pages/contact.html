Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

1. Are Mandi prices updated on Sundays and Holidays?

Most APMC mandis are closed on Sundays and national/state holidays. In such cases, the app displays the latest official closing rates from the previous working session.

2. How does the AI Crop Doctor work?

The AI Crop Doctor analyzes leaf symptoms, discoloration, and fungal patterns from photos you upload or capture with your phone camera, matching against a verified database of 110+ Indian crop diseases and CIBRC-registered remedies.

3. Can I use the app without internet?

Yes, previously fetched mandi prices and your Farm Khata entries are cached locally on your device so you can view them even when you have low or no cellular connectivity in farm fields.

4. How do I report an incorrect mandi price or bug?

Please send an email with the screenshot and Mandi name to edupluscreation@gmail.com, and our technical team will verify and resolve it immediately.
