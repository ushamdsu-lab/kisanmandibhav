Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 20, 2026 | Effective Date: September 20, 2026
Transparency Commitment: Kisan Mandi Bhav ("we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy. We are committed to protecting the privacy and personal data of our users ("you" or "farmers"). This Privacy Policy explains our data collection, usage, and disclosure practices in accordance with Google Play Store Developer Policies.

1. Introduction & Overview

The Kisan Mandi Bhav application (Package Name: com.kisanmitra.kisan_mitra) provides Indian farmers with daily live Agricultural Produce Market Committee (APMC / Mandi) commodity prices, 7-day localized weather forecasts, satellite Doppler radar imagery, an AI-powered Crop Disease Doctor with CIBRC-approved treatments, government agriculture schemes (Yojana), and a digital Farm Khata (ledger).

2. Information We Collect and How We Use It

We believe in data minimization. We only access device capabilities and information necessary to provide and improve the app's features:

3. Advertising & Third-Party SDK Disclosures

To keep our application free and accessible to all farmers, we display advertisements provided by Google AdMob.

Google Mobile Ads (AdMob): AdMob collects and uses device identifiers (Android Advertising ID), general device data, and IP addresses to serve compliant banner advertisements and measure ad performance.

  • You can review Google's Privacy Policy at: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Learn how Google uses data from partner apps: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • You can reset your Advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads anytime from your Android device settings: Settings > Google > Ads > Reset Advertising ID or Delete Advertising ID.

4. Third-Party Services We Integrate

Our app integrates trusted third-party service providers solely to enable essential app features:

  • Google Play Services: Required for application distribution, security, and essential platform APIs.
  • Google Mobile Ads (AdMob): Banner advertising delivery.
  • Open-Meteo & Weather APIs: Public meteorological data for temperature, rainfall, humidity, and wind forecasting.
  • Agmarknet / Enam / State APMC Portals: Public agricultural commodity prices and arrivals information.

5. Data Safety & Security Practices

  • Data Encryption in Transit: All communications between the app and external APIs (such as live mandi rates and weather data) are encrypted using standard industry TLS/HTTPS protocols.
  • No Sale of Personal Information: We do NOT sell, trade, or rent any personal or farming data to data brokers, advertising aggregators, or third parties.
  • Local Data Control: Your sensitive financial entries (Farm Khata) are never transmitted to our servers.

6. Your Rights & Data Deletion

In compliance with Google Play Store's Data Safety requirements, you have full control over your data:

  • Clear Local App Data: You can completely erase all local Khata entries, cached mandi rates, and offline data at any time by going to: Android Settings > Apps > Kisan Mandi Bhav > Storage > Clear Storage / Clear Data.
  • Revoke Permissions: You can grant or revoke Location and Camera permissions at any time via Android Settings. The app will continue to function (allowing manual mandi search) even if location permission is denied.
  • Data Deletion Requests: If you wish to submit a formal data deletion or privacy request, please visit our Data Deletion Policy page or email us at edupluscreation@gmail.com.

7. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Our application is designed for farmers, agricultural professionals, and adult users. The app does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in certain jurisdictions). If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal information to us, please contact us immediately, and we will promptly delete such information.
